## Design Context

### Users
This is a personal homepage for a viewer who wants a quiet, slightly strange
moment: a random Bible verse over a random Wikimedia background, with just
enough controls to pause, inspect, download, or move through recent
backgrounds.

### Brand Personality
Sparse, retro, private. The page should feel like a tiny old-web object that
still has care in the details: monospace text, plain white layers, hidden corner
signals, and controls that reveal themselves only when asked.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the existing design very close to the original simple page. The background
image is the page. UI belongs in small, blunt, low-ceremony white surfaces with
black monospace type. Avoid marketing polish, decorative modern gradients,
rounded app chrome, heavy animation, and anything that makes the page feel like
a product dashboard.

### Design Principles
- Preserve the original sparse composition unless a detail is clearly broken.
- Prefer invisible or nearly invisible controls that become legible on hover or
  keyboard intent.
- Keep typography monospace, simple, and intentionally rough around the edges.
- Let random imagery stay surprising while avoiding broken media experiences.
- Polish through alignment, state clarity, and tiny interaction details rather
  than through visual embellishment.
